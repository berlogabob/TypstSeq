# P25 — sync calm-down (2026-10-04)

## Phone trace before the fix

Measured phone-trace facts supplied for this documentation update:

- Autosave syncs took ~50 s: `scan-local` 30–34 s, `list-remote` 6–7 s,
  and ~80 revision uploads.
- Failures: `Failed host lookup` ×7, `MKCOL 530` ×3, HTTP 502 ×2.

## Fix — f3667ed

- Autosave cloud syncs are throttled to at most once per 2 min; local saving
  remains independent. Note close and backgrounding still trigger sync immediately.
- HTTP 500/502/503/504 and Cloudflare 520–530 retry within a run, alongside
  existing 423/429 retries.
- Transient failures on autosave, poll, background, resume, and startup show
  “Offline — changes saved, will sync” without a sync error. Three consecutive
  failures or 10 min escalate to the normal error; success resets the counters.
  Manual/retry failures remain visible immediately.
- Revision-per-save coalescing was attempted and reverted. It remains a
  follow-up; this fix does not reduce revision creation per save.

No post-fix phone timing was supplied; the ~50 s measurement is the baseline.
